# Honesty rules for every film (v2.3.3, production notes § 3-ب, § 5-ب; CEO decisions 31 Oct 2026 incl. the evening decisions on partner content)

1. **Every on-screen number matches the recording** it comes from (the real screen, the real statement); a number that is not in the source is not on screen. Sample data is marked «بيانات تجريبية · SAMPLE».
2. **No performance promises**; no past-performance implication without the disclaimer; no "guaranteed", "risk-free", "double".
3. **No "signals", no "buy/sell" calls** — the product shows scenarios and analysis ("Scenarios, not signals").
4. Truthful speed claims: "Read any chart, clearly" — never "in seconds" when the thing takes 27 s.
5. Real product only: real screens inside the device frame, zoomed when mentioned until readable; never a generated UI, never a generated face, never the card re-drawn.
6. The fixed risk line on every film as the persistent side line from the first frame to the last (there is no closing card); 9:16 on the left edge, 24 px, muted. **Official content:** the one wording **verbatim, with nothing added inside it** — no entity, no number; the entity line is never burnt into an official video — it is written in the post caption (`caption-template.md`) as «Company registration number» only — 2422384 (Fiper Global UAE, default) or GB23201759 (Fiper Global MAU). **Partner content (tier = person, v2.3.3 — CR-A final form, CEO evening 31 Oct 2026):** the side line carries the entity and the company registration number inside it, in the language of the reel — «الاستثمار ينطوي على مخاطر — Fiper Global، رقم السجل التجاري GB23201759» / "Investing involves risk — Fiper Global, Company registration number GB23201759" — and the number appears in no other element of the frame; the partner caption carries the same entity line. On every surface: never "Licence No." / "License No." / «ترخيص» with any number (the studio's licence wording was refused — the number is right, the wording is not).
7. Person-led reels: no automatic disclaimers like «مثال افتراضي»; an example shown as a statistic, an estimate shown as a fact, or gross fees shown as net profit is a critical finding (`partner-edit.md` § 6.9).
8. **Captions show the spoken words as recorded, never the script's wording** (studio item 11): «سبعة دولارات» stays «سبعة دولارات» if that is what was said; a doubtful word or number goes to a person and is never guessed (`partner-edit.md` § 6.8). The eligibility test treats number words and digits as equal (`take-selection.md`) — the caption does not.
9. **A coloured word is a semantic claim**: profit green / loss red only on a financial value that really is a gain or a loss in the source; an alert or information word takes the amber. Colouring a word red to alarm, or green to sell, is a finding.
10. **No partnership disclosure, no licence-implying title (CR-B / CR-C, CEO 31 Oct 2026):** «بالشراكة مع فايبر», «شراكة مدفوعة», «إعلان», "paid partnership" appear on no surface at any imprint level; the role line of the lower third and the Fiper mention in the caption are the partner's **self-description** (the models of the Scripts Constitution § 4 or the partner's own form); a title implying a licence or a regulatory status the partner does not hold («مستشار مالي مرخص», «محلل معتمد») is a critical finding unless the document is in the partner's profile.
