## Logo by content type (official tier only — CEO 31 Oct 2026; person tier: none)

The base is the horizontal colour lockup A (`svg/fiper-lockup-horizontal-colour.svg`), 96 px high on a 1080 px short side at a 64 px inset, scaled with the short side; the mark alone (`svg/fiper-mark-colour.svg`) only where the lockup cannot keep its minimum width (logos/USAGE.md: lockup ≥ 80 px wide, mark ≥ 16 px, clear space 0.5 × mark height). A soft ground-colour gradient behind the corner keeps the contrast on footage (rgba(10,7,8,.55) → 0). The logo is static after the reveal and never hidden. Values from `logos/USAGE.md`, `component.video.*` and `safe-areas.svg` — nothing outside them.

| Content type | Frame | File | Height (scaled with the short side) | Inset | Corner | Extra offset for the platform UI | Minimum | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reels / Stories 9:16 (Instagram, TikTok, Shorts) | 1080 × 1920 | lockup A horizontal | 96 px | 64 px | top-start (top-right on Arabic-led films) | Stories: below the platform's top bar — add +160 px to the top inset (y = 224 px); Reels: none beyond the 64 px inset; keep the end-side 12 % and the bottom 20 % free of everything else (the side risk line runs on the left edge) | lockup ≥ 80 px wide (never below 28 px high) | gradient behind the corner |
| Film 16:9 (site, YouTube, TV) | 1920 × 1080 | lockup A horizontal | 96 px (short side 1080) | 64 px | top-start | none | lockup ≥ 80 px wide | gradient behind the corner |
| Square 1:1 (feed) | 1080 × 1080 | lockup A horizontal | 96 px | 64 px | top-start | none; keep the bottom 12 % free for the platform's caption area | lockup ≥ 80 px wide | gradient behind the corner |

| In-product / in-site video (app, Hub, Academy) | any | **mark alone** `svg/fiper-mark-colour.svg` when the frame is narrower than 640 px or the product's own header already carries the lockup (Q15 rule); otherwise the lockup | mark 56 px (the UI header value) at frames ≤ 640 px wide; lockup 96 px above | 64 px (≤ 640 px wide: 24 px) | top-start | none — the product chrome is outside the frame | mark ≥ 16 px | gradient behind the corner |

Don't: spinning logo, neon glow pulses, purple/multi-colour gradients, emoji in captions, letter-spaced Arabic, ISO timestamps on screen, stock crowd footage as a hero, a closing card of any kind, a Fiper logo on a partner's personal-brand reel, a brand-red word, a loss-red or profit-green word that is not a financial value, solid pillarbox / letterbox bars, a caption group longer than 26 characters, a lower third longer than 4 s, the risk line on the right edge of a reel, an entity or licence line burnt into OFFICIAL frames, the registration number anywhere on a partner frame except inside the side line, a licence word with any number, a partnership disclosure («بالشراكة مع فايبر», «شراكة مدفوعة», «إعلان», "paid partnership") on any surface, a role line implying a licence the partner does not hold.
