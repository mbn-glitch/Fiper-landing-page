# templates/email — system e-mails (v2.3.4)

Files: `base.html`, `otp.html`, `confirmation.html`, `statement.html` (**Arabic** mails) and `base.en.html`, `otp.en.html`, `confirmation.en.html`, `statement.en.html` (**English** mails) — generated by `node scripts/build-emails.mjs` from one shell; `_regulatory-block.verbatim.html` = the fiper.io footer block, copied, never retyped (one documented correction: the Mauritius company registration number is GB23201759 — the capture carried the typo G823201759; CEO 31 Oct 2026).

Rules
- **One language per mail — the recipient's** (CEO decision 31 Oct 2026, closing the v2.2.0 question): an Arabic recipient gets `*.html`, an English recipient gets `*.en.html`. Each mail carries **ONE equivalence line in the other language** — «This e-mail is also available in English» in the Arabic mail, «هذه الرسالة متاحة بالعربية» in the English mail (`component.email.equivalence-line-en` / `-ar`) — placed after the «why you received this» line, before the footer rule. The full bilingual duplicate block of v2.0–v2.3.1 is gone. The fixed risk line stays bilingual (it is one fixed wording), the entity line and the regulatory block stay in English (approved texts are never translated or retyped).
- **Paper ground in every mode** (CEO decision 31 Oct 2026): `color-scheme: light only`, no `prefers-color-scheme: dark` block, no `[data-ogsc]` rules, no white-logo swap. Why: mail clients apply dark schemes unpredictably — Gmail inverts some colours and strips some media queries, Outlook rewrites them, Apple Mail honours them — so a dark variant can neither be controlled nor tested from the package; the paper ground stays legible under whatever inversion a client applies. Light / dark modes belong to sites and apps only (`README.md` § J; `component.email.scheme` = paper-only). Gate: `check-emails` fails any dark rule in a mail template.
- Dark official lockup raster **on the paper ground** (`logos/email/email-header-160-paper@2x.png`, 320 × 112 → 160 × 56 CSS px) — the only header image (`component.email.logo-file`). **v2.3.4 (invoices change request 1 / FI7-017):** the previous `email-header-160@2x.png` sat on an opaque WHITE box over the paper shell in every paper mail of the system; the header is now flattened on the paper token `#F7F4F1` (`semantic.color.light.bg`). Why the flattened raster and not the transparent twin: a client that inverts the mail (Gmail dark) keeps a paper rectangle with a legible dark wordmark, while a transparent dark wordmark would vanish on the inverted ground; both variants ship (`-transparent[@2x].png` for a header on a white card). Gates: `check-emails` decodes the header and fails an opaque white corner or the old file name; `verify-identity` OLD-EMAIL-HEADER; proof render `dist/checks/email/header-paper-proof.png`.
- Every link lives under a button only; visible text never contains a URL (gate: tag-strip + grep).
- Arabic in IBM Plex Sans Arabic with real weights (fallback 'Segoe UI', Tahoma, Arial), English in Inter (same fallbacks), `font-synthesis: none`; monospace only on codes and amounts.
- Entity line: «**Company registration number**» only — Fiper Global UAE 2422384 by default; the MAU alternative «Fiper Global MAU — Company registration number: GB23201759» is selectable in `templates/legal/regulatory-block.html`; never "Licence No." / "License No." with any number (`verify-identity` LICENCE-WORDING + REG-NUMBER-WORDING).
- Preview: Chromium at 360 and 390 px, light only (`scripts/check-templates.mjs` → `dist/checks/email/*-light.png`). Real clients: `TEST-MATRIX.md` — NOT TESTED.
