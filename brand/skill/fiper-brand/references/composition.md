# Composition — Fiper Brand v2.2.0 (approved direction, short form; full text `brand/references/composition-rules.md`)

1. **Two grounds**: paper `#F7F4F1` for documents, e-mails, educational/corporate posts, presentations; charcoal `--fp-ground-bg` is the DEFAULT of every app, site and marketing surface. The light theme for apps is APPROVED as OPTIONAL (CEO 30 Oct 2026): complete set under `[data-theme="light"]`, user/system toggle, every component renders in both; marketing stays charcoal.
2. **Constant core**: official lockup top-start (white SVG on charcoal, dark raster PNG on paper); Arabic headline IBM Plex Sans Arabic 700; English secondary Inter 500 muted; numbers IBM Plex Mono tabular right-aligned; 1 px rules between levels; meta foot (entity · reference · date · SAMPLE) bottom-start.
3. **Three poles, pick one**: image-dominant (card ad, product post) · headline-dominant (report cover, educational comparison, corporate post, letterhead) · number-dominant (portfolio, slide, report inner page, statements, dashboards). Never two dominant elements, never a repeated single template.
4. **Margin = 2 × lockup height** per medium: social 72–84 px, slide 112 px, A4 64 px (17 mm), e-mail 28–40 px; phone gutter 20 px. One vertical start line per surface.
5. **Cards only to group or to bound an action** (e-mail facts table, phone chart frame, sheets, selectable fund cards). Panel fill, 1 px border, 12–16 px radius, no shadow.
6. **Red roles**: logo tile · the one primary action · active state (range marker, active tab, current-value dot) **+ the exempt finance semantics**: loss ▼ word red `--fp-finance-loss` (#F05252 / light #B91C1C), gain ▲ word green, pending clock amber, neutral grey — a red figure is never a button, numbers in Plex Mono, never on a filled red ground, no red text otherwise (gates RED-FIGURE, LOSS-IN-BRAND-RED, NUM-ON-RED). Production rules Q1–Q17 (video corner 96/64 on every frame of official content — no closing card and no logo on person-led reels since v2.3.1, fixed risk line «الاستثمار ينطوي على مخاطر» / "Investing involves risk" — in film a side line on the LEFT edge of reels at 24 px muted, in apps the foot of the sign-in screen and of every screen with financial figures (v2.3.2), e-mail logo 160 × 56, story margin 96, divider `--fp-line-divider`, asset types screen/artwork, QR ≥ 160 px recommended 12 % never above 300 px, no product label under shots, lower third once ≤ 4 s in total (centre-bottom at 80 % on reels), logo alone < 640 px, image grade 8 %; video emphasis is the one exception to «no red words»: profit / loss colours on financial values only, alert words amber): `brand/references/composition-rules.md` § 11.
7. **Readable support**: social body ≥ 42 px on 1080 (≈ 14 px at 360), phone body 14–15 px, A4 body 14 px, slides ≥ 26 px. Shorten or split; never shrink.
8. **Charts from data**: `brand/scripts/charts.mjs` + `brand/scripts/sample-data.mjs` only; positions, axes, labels, benchmark computed; title, period, currency, end value shown.
9. **Unbreakable units**: amount+currency, sign+number+percent, date parts in `.fp-unit` / `<bdi class="num">` (nowrap, ltr, isolate). Test HTML, PDF, exports.
10. **No slash label prefix, no decorative numbering, no icon on headings or in tiles**; Lucide for navigation/state/action only.

Commands (run from the folder that contains `brand/`): `node brand/scripts/build-tokens.mjs` · `node brand/scripts/verify-tokens.mjs` · `node brand/scripts/contrast.mjs` (writes `./checks/contrast.json`) · `node brand/scripts/verify-identity.mjs brand src public`.
