# Reviewer prompt — automated film review with the FULL identity (v2.3.3)

Person-led reels (a real person talking to camera): give the reviewer this file **and** the person-led prompt in `partner-edit.md` § 10-ب (editing, presence, on-screen text, audio, compliance for the person tier) — it adds to this file, never replaces it. The writing rules are the Scripts Constitution's (`partner-edit.md` § 17), not this file's.

Give the reviewing model the complete rule set below, never a summary ("red only for logo and button" made it flag the real trading screen's red and ask to alter a real recording — forbidden). Then **verify every note on real frames** before any correction.

## Rules the reviewer must apply
- Ground `#0A0708` everywhere; shadows `rgba(10,7,8,x)`, never pure black. Text `#EDEAE6` / secondary `#8E8681`. Light theme never on marketing films.
- Red `#DC2626` = the logo tile and the ONE small CTA pill (official content only, over the last shot — never a card); no brand-red words, flashes, glows. **No red words in UI, sites, apps and static design.** In video, **semantic emphasis is allowed when it serves the meaning, with three values only and by role**: **profit `#2AB37A` and loss `#F05252` only on a financial VALUE in the line** (a number, a percentage, an amount with its currency); **alert / information words — «مخاطر», «الرسوم» — amber `#D9A441`**; at most one coloured word per caption group; `#DC2626` is never used for words (CEO 31 Oct 2026; studio item 5). A loss-red or profit-green WORD is a finding. **Exempt: red inside a real trading screen or app recording (it is the product, never altered), the finance red of a real loss figure (`#F05252`, word + sign + Plex Mono, never a button).**
- Type: Barlow Condensed 700 uppercase (letter-spacing .5 px, line-height 1.0, 110–230 px on 1080 width, start margin 84 px) for Latin headlines; Inter 600 uppercase .06em 32–40 px secondary; IBM Plex Mono 600 tabular numbers; IBM Plex Sans Arabic 600/700 for Arabic, letter-spacing 0, never a mono or Latin font on Arabic.
- Motion: `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`, reveal 0.8 s with a 26 px offset, no bounce, no flash; x/y/scale only; scene transitions = ground-colour fade 0.9→0 in 0.24 s; corner logo 96 px at 64 px inset (official content only), reveal from the first frame — mark 0.3 s then word +0.15 s, static after, **present on every frame, never hidden**; grain 0.04–0.06 at 24 fps steps; vignette ≤ 0.45 to the ground colour.
- Ending: **no closing card in any tier** (CEO 31 Oct 2026) — the film ends on the speaker (or the last shot) with the takeaway: ≤ 8 words over the picture, readable 0.5 s + 0.3 s/word (≥ 1.5 s), **76 px inside 130 px side margins on a 9:16 frame at 1080 wide**, one CTA at most (the small red pill on official content only); **official content: the entity line is NEVER on the frames** (it is in the caption: «Company registration number» only); **partner content (v2.3.3): the entity and the company registration number appear ONLY inside the side risk line** — in any other element they are a finding; never "Licence No." / "License No." / «ترخيص» with any number anywhere; a stacked lockup, a tagline card, fiper.me on a card, or any end screen is a finding.
- Logo by tier: official content — the corner lockup on every frame; **person-led content — NO Fiper logo anywhere** (a logo on a partner reel is a finding).
- Risk line as a SMALL PERSISTENT SIDE line for the whole film, **first frame to last frame, in both tiers** — **official: «الاستثمار ينطوي على مخاطر · Investing involves risk» verbatim, nothing added inside it; partner (person tier, v2.3.3 — CR-A final form): «الاستثمار ينطوي على مخاطر — Fiper Global، رقم السجل التجاري GB23201759» on an Arabic reel / "Investing involves risk — Fiper Global, Company registration number GB23201759" on an English reel, one language (the reel's), verbatim — any other wording (a licence word, «MAU», a bilingual line, a missing entity) is a finding**: **9:16 — rotated along the LEFT frame edge inside the safe margin (the right edge collides with the Reels UI), 24 px at 1080 wide, muted at the divider token rgba(237,234,230,.28)**; 16:9 / 1:1 — bottom-start corner, ≥ 22 px at 1920; never red; never over the subject or headline — missing from a frame (including the first and the last), on the right edge of a reel, in the full text colour, or hidden under a lower third is a finding; hidden only during colliding logo-reveal frames.
- Logo only from the approved set (variant A, dotted i, filter-free SVG): never the dotless-i file, never mirrored, never a mark crop.
- Real product first: screens inside the 4 px bezel frame, artwork (the card) full bleed without a frame; no generated faces, screens, logos, neon, purple, crimson accents.
- **A source that is not 9:16** (or not the frame's aspect) is shown complete **over a blurred copy of itself** — solid pillarbox / letterbox bars or a gradient band are a finding (studio item 7).
- Audio: −14.0…−14.2 LUFS / TP ≤ −1.5; music ducked −9…−12 dB under the WHOLE speech span; no game/motivational/documentary/horror/epic-build music; **person-led content: no music by default until licences are bought**; English VO per `voice-over.md`; Arabic VO human only.
- Claims: every number matches its recording; no promises, no signals, no buy/sell. **Caption text = the spoken words as recorded** — a caption that shows the script's wording instead of what was said, or a number that differs from the audio, is a critical finding (studio item 11; `take-selection.md`).
- **No partnership disclosure on any surface (CR-B)**: «بالشراكة مع فايبر», «شراكة مدفوعة», «إعلان», "paid partnership", "#ad" in the lower third, on the image or in the caption is a finding; **the role line is the partner's self-description (CR-C)** — a title implying a licence or a regulatory status the partner does not hold («مستشار مالي مرخص», «محلل معتمد») is a critical finding unless documented in the profile.
- Safe area: no text outside 90 %; **lower third once, ≤ 4 s on screen in total (entrance and exit included); on 9:16 centred at the bottom with its bottom edge at 80 % of the height** (studio item 6 — a start-edge block at 17 % or a 5 s hold is a finding); no product label under a supporting shot; captions one line, split by meaning, **≤ 26 characters per group**, ≤ 5 words per group on person-led reels; **the caption group that repeats the takeaway text must be hidden while the takeaway is on** (studio item 10) and every caption hidden under the single title card.

## Known failure modes of the automated reviewer (treat its notes as hypotheses)
1. Counted the allowed red of a real trading screen / environment as a violation and asked to alter a real recording.
2. Claimed heavy slow-motion artefacts that the frames did not show.
3. Claimed the music stopped at "FREE"; the measurement proved it did not.
4. Scored eight voices 10/10 — scoring one by one does not discriminate; demand a ranking.
5. Mis-timed a beat; beats are detected by low-frequency energy, not by ear.

## Output the reviewer must give
Timestamped findings (mm:ss.f), the rule each one breaks, severity, and a frame reference; then a person checks each finding on the exported frame board (every 0.5–1 s) before anything is changed.
