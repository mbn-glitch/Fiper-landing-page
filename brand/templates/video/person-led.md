# Person-led content — CEO or partner reels (v2.3.3; the CEO's 14 Oct 2026 direction, verbatim in substance, the CEO decisions of 31 Oct 2026 and the Partners Studio change requests 1–12; composition rule § 12)

**The editing reference for every partner / personal-brand reel is `partner-edit.md`** (cuts and pauses, take selection — with `take-selection.md` for the number normalisation and the merge of adjacent cuts —, punch-ins, hook and takeaway, inserts, on-screen text, the phone-dialogue chain, music, the two tiers, acceptance, the script → edit contract, the stop-and-reshoot rules) with its values in `tokens.json` → `video.person_edit` and its gate `scripts/check-partner-edit.mjs`. This file is the short form. The writing rules (idea, structure, dialect, numbers, claims, prohibitions) belong to the Partners Studio Scripts Constitution (`fiper-creative-studio` → `docs/SCRIPT_CONSTITUTION_AR.md`, `partner-edit.md` § 17).

Two content tiers (`tier` in the script, never decided by the engine; **G-Q4, CEO 31 Oct 2026: every script that comes through the Partners Studio is `tier=person` — no logo — whatever the imprint level 0–3 or the partner's role, Fiper employees and office managers included; `tier=official` only for content the company produces itself**): **official company content** = the full identity (this kit as is); **person-led content** = the person's presence first, the identity at its minimum:
- **No Fiper logo on the video at all** (CEO 31 Oct 2026) — no corner badge, no mark, no stacked lockup. The light touches only: IBM Plex Sans Arabic, the caption style, the semantic colours, the persistent side risk line, the partner's lower third once (`LowerThird`).
- **No closing card** (CEO 31 Oct 2026). The reel ends on the speaker with the **takeaway**: a short text over the speaker (≤ 8 words, readable 0.5 s + 0.3 s/word, ≥ 1.5 s; **76 px inside 130 px side margins at 1080 wide**), one CTA at most as a plain line; the side risk line runs to the last frame.
- **The side risk line** **on the LEFT edge of the reel** (the right edge collides with the Reels UI), **24 px at 1080 wide, muted at the divider token**, from the first frame to the last — **v2.3.3 (CR-A final form, CEO evening 31 Oct 2026): on partner content the line carries the entity and the company registration number inside it, in the language of the reel** — «الاستثمار ينطوي على مخاطر — Fiper Global، رقم السجل التجاري GB23201759» (Arabic reel) / "Investing involves risk — Fiper Global, Company registration number GB23201759" (English reel); one language, never bilingual on partner content; the number nowhere else on the frame; never «ترخيص» / License (the licence wording stays refused). `RiskLine({ tier:'person', lang })`.
- **The lower third** once after the hook: **centred at the bottom, its bottom edge at 80 % of the height, ≤ 4 s on screen in total** including the entrance and the exit (studio item 6). **Its role line is the partner's self-description** (CR-C — the models by partner role: employee / office manager «[المنصب] في فايبر» · «مدير مكتب فايبر — [المدينة]» · «فريق فايبر»; identified broker «وكيل معتمد لشركة فايبر» · «شريك استراتيجي لفايبر» · «شريك فايبر الرسمي في [البلد]»; strategy provider «مزود استراتيجية في فايبر» · «استراتيجيتي متاحة للنسخ في فايبر»; content creator by agreement «شريك محتوى مع فايبر» · «بالتعاون مع فايبر»; or the partner's own form — the one legal limit: no title implying a licence or a regulatory status the partner does not hold). **No partnership disclosure on any surface** (CR-B): no «بالشراكة مع فايبر», no «شراكة مدفوعة», no «إعلان», no "paid partnership" — `LowerThird` throws, the gates fail.
- Captions from the first frame, split by meaning, one line, **≤ 26 characters per group**, **the spoken words as recorded — never the script**; at most one emphasised word per group in a semantic colour: **profit green / loss red only on a financial value**, alert and information words (e.g. «مخاطر») amber #D9A441 — never brand red. **The caption group that repeats the takeaway text is hidden while the takeaway is on** (G7 exception).
- **A source that is not 9:16** (a 16:9 phone clip, a landscape screen) is shown complete over a **blurred copy of itself** — never solid bars (studio item 7).
- **No music by default until licences are bought** (CEO 31 Oct 2026); a strong dialogue-only version first.
- **The entity on partner content lives inside the side risk line and in the caption** (`caption-template.md` person section: «Fiper Global — رقم السجل التجاري GB23201759» / «Fiper Global — Company registration number GB23201759», the Mauritius entity only, no UAE line) — never in any other element of the frame. Official content keeps the entity in the caption only.

The direction (14 Oct):
- Character: a contemporary financial expert speaking directly. No games, no slide show, no motivational-ad tone.
- Visual motion: no cartoon characters, no rubbery motion; **editorial, analytical graphics** instead — not black-and-gold stiffness.
- The person is the main element: alone, or with a terse caption, or a full graphic only when explaining more — then back to the person.
- Editing: **one question per reel**, a true strong opening, no repetition; **keep the expressive pauses, never speed up the voice**; automatic cutting of every pause is forbidden (it kills the rhythm).
- Audio: never generate speech in the person's voice, never recombine their words into a new sentence without consent — re-record a short line instead. Adjacent cuts of ONE continuous take merged before the eligibility test are not a recombination (`take-selection.md`).
- Music: measured movement, curiosity, confidence; it does not need to run the whole reel — and none by default until licensed.
- Development: a strong **dialogue-only version first**, **one CTA at most**, and an **A/B sample** before redoing a whole set in a new direction.

Review: `review-prompt.md` (the full identity) **plus** the person-led reviewer prompt in `partner-edit.md` § 10-ب; the first five edits of every new partner go to the CEO or a delegate before compliance. Sample: `examples/film-person-9x16.html` → `samples/fiper-film-person-9x16.webm` (built from the library; the speaker footage is stood in by a real device shot because the package carries no speaker clip and nothing may be generated in anyone's face or voice).
