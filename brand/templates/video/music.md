# Music rules — Fiper video (v2.3.1, from production notes § 3-ب, § 5-ج and the CEO's standing direction)

- **Beat detection by numbers**, not by a reviewer's ear: low-frequency energy over time gives the drum hits; the **first hit lands on the logo or the main promise, the second on the message peak** (e.g. "FREE"). Cut and slow the track (≈ 3 %) to place the hits; the proven structure: 8 bars, then the last 4 bars of the build-up.
- **Banned** (CEO standing direction): game / arcade music, generic motivational, documentary tone, horror, big cinematic build-ups. Wanted: measured movement, curiosity, confidence; music does not have to run the whole piece — it may stop.
- **Lyria allowed** (original, rights-free) with **3–4 candidates compared** — its default character drifts to sports music; pick by comparison.
- Levels and ducking: `audio-standards.md` (−20 LUFS bed, −9…−12 dB under the whole speech span, 0.3 s ramps).
- **No music by default in any person-led video until the licences are bought** (CEO 31 Oct 2026; `partner-edit.md` § 8 — no approved licensed list exists; Warner Chappell Production Music / Intervox are the candidate libraries, the purchase is the CEO's). When licensed: optional, never continuous, never over the speaker's pauses (`person-led.md`); an unsuitable track is never "fixed" by lowering it.
